# Storefront

A worked example of the public catalog page, built only from the components.

1. NavBar, dark.
2. Hero on a `data-theme="dark"` band: an eyebrow, `display-xl` with one word under the volt highlighter (`fhd-mark`), a `lead`, and two `lg` Buttons with one `primary`. The category index beside it uses `display-sm` rows under a `border-thick` rule.
3. Catalog on `bg`: a `display-lg` heading, Chips to filter, and ProductCards four across on a `space-6` gap.
4. Contact on a `bg-subtle` band: the form on `bg-raised`, two fields to a row, ending in one `primary` `lg` Button, with the privacy line beside it.
5. Footer on a dark band with the stacked Wordmark.

Sections are `space-20` tall top and bottom; the hero is `space-24`. Content stays inside `container`. Every product, price and count on it is sample data.
